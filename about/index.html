What we do

Two sides of one bench.

Equipment one organisation has finished with is often exactly what another is looking for. We sit in between: we buy it, check it part by part, wipe every drive and grade it honestly, so it can go back to work. What can’t be reused goes to recyclers in our partner network.

Selling or retiring IT

Why we do it

The greenest laptop is usually the one that already exists.

Every device we put back into service is one less that has to be made from scratch. And hardware that has reached the end of its life on one company’s books usually still has value in the market, so the business that lets it go gets some of that value back.

When something truly can’t be reused, we hand it to one of the recyclers in our partner network.

Reuse first, dispose responsibly
