IT asset disposal for businesses

Retiring old IT equipment? We’ll take it from here.

Hardware that’s reached the end of its life on your books usually still has value in the market. We collect it, wipe it, and either refurbish it for resale or pass it to a recycler from our partner network — and you get a clear account of what happened to every unit.

How it works

  1. 01

    List it

    Send us what you have — models, quantities, rough condition. A spreadsheet is fine. So is a photo of a storeroom.

  2. 02

    We collect

    Packed and logged at your site, so every unit is accounted for from the moment it leaves.

  3. 03

    Data wiped

    Every drive sanitized and verified before anything else happens to it.

  4. 04 — Reuse

    Value back to you

    Units with life left are refurbished and resold, so you recover value from equipment you’d already written off.

  5. 04 — Recycle

    Responsible disposal

    If something can’t be reused, we hand it to a recycler from our partner network, who takes care of its disposal.

What we take

Single lots or whole fleets.

We buy used IT equipment from businesses, institutions and dealers. Send us a list and we’ll come back with an offer.

  • Laptops
  • Desktops
  • Servers
  • Monitors
  • Networking
  • Accessories

Note the condition as best you can — working, mixed, or for parts only. Anything that can’t be refurbished or resold goes to one of the recyclers in our partner network.

Your data

The last owner’s data stays with the last owner.

Every storage device is wiped, and the wipe is verified, before the unit is refurbished, resold or handed to a recycler. Nothing from a machine’s previous life survives into its next one.

  • Full overwrite
  • Verification pass
  • Logged per device

If you need records for your own compliance, ask and we’ll walk you through what we provide. Taken the drives out already? Just say so in your enquiry.

Sustainability

Reuse first. Dispose responsibly.

The greenest laptop is usually the one that already exists. Every device we put back into service is one less that has to be made from scratch — and when something truly can’t be reused, we hand it to one of the recyclers in our partner network.
